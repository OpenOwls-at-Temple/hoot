##  Fitness and Sports Facilities

Faculty and staff can take advantage of Temple's state-of-the-art fitness facilities, including weight rooms, climbing walls, indoor tracks and courts, pools and more.

Employee Health Services offers many primary-care services, including allergy shots, immunizations and physicals.
