## Human Resources

Temple University Human Resources is comprised of several divisions including labor and employee relations, employment services, benefits administration, compensation, and more. [Learn about Human Resources at Temple](https://careers.temple.edu/about-hr/what-we-do).

Benefits

Human Resources works to enhance its competitive, comprehensive benefits package. [Find information about employee benefits Temple offers](https://careers.temple.edu/hr-resources/our-functional-areas/benefits-administration "Link opens in new browser window.").

Compensation

Temple focuses on internal equity and external competitiveness when it comes to compensation. [Learn about the salary structure at Temple](https://careers.temple.edu/hr-resources/our-functional-areas/compensation).

Employee Manual

Temple sets forth its expected standards for conduct and performance. [See information about the university's standards for employee conduct](https://careers.temple.edu/hr-resources/our-functional-areas/labor-and-employee-relations/employee-manual-and-rules-conduct).

Organizational Hierarchy

Temple relies on organizational hierarchy for numerous programs related to employment, including the Performance Development and Salary Increase systems. [Learn about Temple's organizational hierarchy](https://careers.temple.edu/hr-resources/our-functional-areas/compensation/organizational-hierarchy-tools).

Payroll

[Find information about payroll management](https://careers.temple.edu/hr-resources/our-functional-areas/payroll-management) at Temple University.
