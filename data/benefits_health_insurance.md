# Health Insurance Benefits at Temple University

Temple University provides comprehensive health insurance options to eligible full-time faculty and staff through Independence Blue Cross (IBX). Coverage begins on the first day of the month following your date of hire.

## Medical Plan Options

### 1. Keystone HMO
A Health Maintenance Organization plan. You must select a primary care physician (PCP) who coordinates all your care. Referrals are required to see specialists. The HMO has the lowest monthly premium of the three plans. In-network care only, except for emergencies.

- No deductible for in-network care
- Copay: $20 for primary care visits, $40 for specialist visits
- Prescription drug copays: $10 generic / $30 brand-name preferred / $50 non-preferred

### 2. Personal Choice PPO
A Preferred Provider Organization plan. You may see any doctor or specialist without a referral. In-network providers cost less; out-of-network care is covered at a reduced rate.

- Annual deductible: $250 individual / $500 family (in-network)
- Coinsurance: 80% in-network after deductible / 60% out-of-network
- Copay: $25 primary care / $50 specialist (in-network)
- Prescription drug copays: $15 generic / $40 brand-name preferred / $65 non-preferred

### 3. High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with Health Savings Account (HSA)
A plan with lower monthly premiums and a higher annual deductible. Paired with a Health Savings Account that allows you to save pre-tax dollars for qualified medical expenses. Temple contributes to your HSA each year.

- Annual deductible: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family
- Coinsurance: 80% in-network after deductible
- Temple HSA contribution: $500 individual / $1,000 family per calendar year
- Prescription drugs subject to deductible, then 80% coinsurance

## Enrollment and Eligibility

- **Eligibility:** Full-time faculty and staff are eligible from their first day of employment. Part-time employees working 20+ hours per week may qualify — contact HR for details.
- **Coverage start date:** The first day of the month following your hire date. Example: hired October 14 → coverage begins November 1.
- **Dependents:** You may enroll your spouse or domestic partner and dependent children up to age 26.
- **Open enrollment:** Held each November for coverage changes effective January 1.
- **Qualifying life events:** Marriage, birth, adoption, or loss of other coverage allows a 30-day special enrollment window outside of open enrollment.

## Dental and Vision

Temple offers separate dental coverage through Delta Dental and vision coverage through Davis Vision. Enrollment is optional and independent of medical plan selection.

- **Delta Dental PPO:** Preventive care (cleanings, exams) at 100%; basic services at 80%; major services at 50% after deductible.
- **Davis Vision:** Annual eye exam covered at 100%; allowance for frames or contact lenses.

## Continuing Coverage (COBRA)

If you leave Temple or lose eligibility, you may continue coverage under COBRA for up to 18 months by paying the full premium plus a 2% administrative fee. Contact the Benefits Office within 60 days of your coverage loss to elect COBRA.
