Temple's faculty, centers and institutes receive tens of millions of dollars in funding each year to continue or initiate innovative research projects that have the ability to change the world as we know it. Support comes from a variety of public and private sources, including foundations, government agencies, corporations and personal philanthropy.

We offer a wide array of support to help students, staff and faculty locate and secure research funding.
