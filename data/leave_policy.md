# Leave Policy at Temple University

## Vacation Leave

Full-time non-bargaining employees receive paid vacation based on salary grade:

- **Grade T21–T25:** 15 days per year after completing one year of service; increases to 20 days after 12 years of service.
- **Grade T26 and above:** 20 days per year from the start.
- **15+ years of service (effective July 1, 2023):** Up to 25 days per year.

Employees may roll over up to half of their annual vacation entitlement each fiscal year. Unused vacation above the rollover limit is forfeited.

## Sick Leave

Full-time non-bargaining employees receive 10 sick days per year. During the probationary period, sick days accrue at one day per month. Unused sick days roll into a paid sick leave bank with no cap. Employees who retire with at least 100 banked days receive a payout for 30 of those days (effective July 1, 2023).

## Bereavement Leave

Paid bereavement leave for non-bargaining employees:

- **4 days:** Spouse, parent, child, dependent stepchild, sister, or brother
- **3 days:** Grandparent, grandchild, father-in-law, or mother-in-law
- **1 day:** Grandparent-in-law, sister-in-law, brother-in-law, son-in-law, or daughter-in-law

Leave may be taken between the date of passing and the day after the ceremony or burial.

## Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)

To qualify for FMLA you must have:
- At least 12 months of service with Temple over the past seven years
- Worked at least 1,250 hours in the past 12 months

Eligible employees may take up to **12 workweeks of unpaid, job-protected leave** per year for:
- Birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child
- Recovery from a serious personal health condition
- Caring for a seriously ill spouse, child, or parent
- A qualifying military exigency

FMLA leave may be taken continuously, intermittently, or on a reduced schedule. Health benefits continue during FMLA leave under the same terms as if you were still working.

## How to Request Leave

1. Notify your supervisor as early as possible.
2. For FMLA or extended medical leave, contact the **Benefits Office at 215-926-2282** to obtain the required forms.
3. Complete and return the leave request form before your leave begins (or as soon as practicable for emergencies).
4. For sick leave, notify your supervisor before your shift starts when possible.

## Personal Days

Full-time employees receive **3 personal days per fiscal year**. Personal days do not roll over and must be used by June 30 each year.
