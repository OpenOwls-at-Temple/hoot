# Leave Policy at Temple University

## How to Request Leave

To request any type of leave at Temple University, follow these steps:

1. **Notify your supervisor** as early as possible — before your leave begins whenever possible.
2. **For sick leave (1–2 days):** Notify your supervisor before your shift starts. No forms required for short absences.
3. **For planned vacation:** Submit a request to your supervisor in advance. Most departments require at least one week notice for vacation days.
4. **For extended medical leave or FMLA:** Contact the Benefits Office at 215-926-2282 to obtain the required leave request forms.
5. **Complete and submit the forms** before your leave begins, or as soon as practicable in emergencies.
6. **HR will confirm** your leave approval and track your leave balance.

## Vacation Leave

Full-time non-bargaining employees receive paid vacation based on salary grade:

- **Grade T21–T25:** 15 days per year after completing one year of service; increases to 20 days after 12 years.
- **Grade T26 and above:** 20 days per year from the start.
- **15+ years of service (effective July 1, 2023):** Up to 25 days per year.

Employees may roll over up to half of their annual vacation entitlement each fiscal year.

## Sick Leave

Full-time non-bargaining employees receive 10 sick days per year. During the probationary period, sick days accrue at one day per month. Unused sick days roll into a paid sick leave bank with no cap.

## Personal Days

Full-time employees receive 3 personal days per fiscal year. Personal days do not roll over and must be used by June 30 each year.

## Bereavement Leave

Paid bereavement leave for non-bargaining employees:

- **4 days:** Spouse, parent, child, dependent stepchild, sister, or brother
- **3 days:** Grandparent, grandchild, father-in-law, or mother-in-law
- **1 day:** Grandparent-in-law, sister-in-law, brother-in-law, son-in-law, or daughter-in-law

## Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)

To qualify you must have at least 12 months of service and worked 1,250 hours in the past year. Eligible employees may take up to 12 workweeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year for:

- Birth or adoption of a child
- Recovery from a serious personal health condition
- Caring for a seriously ill family member
- A qualifying military exigency
