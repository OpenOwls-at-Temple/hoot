# Temple University Tuition Remission Policy

## Overview

Temple University offers a tuition remission benefit to eligible full-time faculty and staff. This benefit covers tuition costs for courses taken at Temple University.

## Eligibility

Full-time employees who have completed at least six months of continuous service are eligible for tuition remission. Part-time employees working at least 50% of a full-time schedule may be eligible for pro-rated benefits.

## Coverage

- Undergraduate courses: up to 18 credits per academic year
- Graduate courses: up to 9 credits per academic year
- Dependent children of eligible employees may receive tuition remission for undergraduate programs

## Tax Implications

Graduate tuition remission may be taxable above the IRS-defined limit of $5,250 per calendar year. Employees should consult with the Office of Human Resources for specific tax guidance.

## How to Apply

1. Complete the Tuition Remission Application form available on TUportal
2. Obtain supervisor approval
3. Submit to the Office of Human Resources at least two weeks before the start of classes

## Contact

For questions about tuition remission, contact the Office of Human Resources at 215-204-7174 or visit the HR website.

# Temple University Leave Policies

## Paid Time Off

Full-time faculty and staff accrue paid time off based on years of service:

- 0-5 years: 15 days per year
- 5-10 years: 20 days per year
- 10+ years: 25 days per year

## Sick Leave

Employees accrue one sick day per month of service. Unused sick leave carries over year to year with no cap.

## Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)

Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year for qualified family and medical reasons. Employees must have worked at least 1,250 hours in the preceding 12 months to qualify.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member.

## Contact

For questions about leave policies, contact the Office of Human Resources at 215-204-7174.

# Temple University Code of Conduct

## Overview

All Temple University employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of professional and ethical conduct. This code applies to faculty, staff, and student workers.

## Key Principles

- Act with integrity and honesty in all university-related activities
- Treat all members of the university community with respect and dignity
- Protect university resources and use them responsibly
- Report any suspected violations of university policy
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or professional interests that could conflict with their university duties. Disclosure forms must be submitted annually to the Office of the Provost.

## Reporting Violations

Suspected violations of the Code of Conduct should be reported to the Office of Human Resources or through the university's anonymous ethics hotline at 1-888-384-5575.

## Consequences

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

## Contact

For questions about the Code of Conduct, contact the Office of Human Resources at 215-204-7174.
